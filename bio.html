Short Bio

Chaosheng Dong is a Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon. He received his Ph.D. in Operations Research from the University of Pittsburgh in 2020, and his B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) in 2014.

His research interests include multi-objective optimization, multi-task learning, federated learning, and learning to rank, with applications in e-commerce search and recommendation.
